01 — About

About Me

Dr. Suresh Purini is an Associate Professor at the International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad (IIIT-Hyderabad), where he heads the Computer Systems Group. His research interests span compilers, computer architecture, and parallel and distributed systems. His current research focuses on the design of domain-specific accelerators — particularly machine-learning accelerators — and agentic AI systems for hardware accelerator development targeting FPGAs and ASICs.

He is also a certified Yoga Trainer and a Heartfulness Meditation Trainer, and is actively engaged in promoting heart-centered education at the school, college, and community levels.
